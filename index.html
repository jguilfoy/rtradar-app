About RTRadar

RTRadar shows live data from the U.S. NEXRAD weather radar network: the radar nearest you and its neighbors, blended into one map, with loops, wind views, lightning, and a 3D view of storms over terrain.

RTRadar is not an official warning service. For watches and warnings, follow the National Weather Service and your local officials. Rotation markers are detected from radar data and can be wrong.

Common questions

Why does part of the map look blocky? While nearby radars load, the app shows NOAA's national mosaic underneath so the map is never blank. Detailed radar replaces it within seconds.

How much data does it use? Settings shows data used this session and lets you choose how much to use on cellular (Good, Better, Best). On Wi-Fi the app always uses Best.

Does it need my location? No. Location only centers the map and picks the nearest radar, and it never leaves your device. Without it, search for any place in Settings.

Data sources

Radar: NOAA NEXRAD Level II and MRMS. Lightning: NOAA GOES Geostationary Lightning Mapper. All via the AWS Open Data program. Terrain: Mapzen/Tilezen Terrain Tiles (USGS 3DEP, SRTM, GMTED2010, ETOPO1 and others). Maps: Apple.
