Privacy Policy

RTRadar · Effective October 26, 2026
In short: RTRadar does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

RTRadar is published by Ignite Development LLC ("we"). Contact: jeff@ignitedevllc.com.

Your location

If you allow it, RTRadar uses your device's location while the app is open to center the map and choose the nearest radar. Your location is used only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not stored. You can turn location off at any time in iOS Settings; the app still works if you search for a place.

Weather data the app downloads

RTRadar downloads public weather and terrain files directly from NOAA's and Mapzen's public datasets hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS Open Data). Like any web request, these downloads reveal your device's IP address to AWS, and the files requested (for example, a particular radar station's data) reflect the general region you are viewing. We do not operate these servers and receive none of this information. Maps and place search are provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your settings (such as map layer, loop length and data-use preference) and the last area you viewed, on your device only, so the app opens where you left it. Deleting the app deletes them.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone.

Children

RTRadar does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
